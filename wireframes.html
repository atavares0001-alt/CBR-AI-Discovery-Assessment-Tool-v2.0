“Under the Hood” — Layout Concepts

Four alternative slide designs for the automation status, time drains, and magic-wand data. Each is shown at 16:9 aspect ratio using the exact project dark theme.

1 Full-Width Vertical Flow

Single-column, top-to-bottom hierarchy. The slide title sits at top-left with a compact header, followed by a full-width automation banner, then a two-card row for the pain points. Feels clean and editorial — easy to scan quickly.
